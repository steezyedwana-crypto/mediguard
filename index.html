Detects providers where the ratio of Item 36 (long) to Item 23 (standard) claims sits more than 2 standard deviations from peer cohort. Checks consultation timestamp from Pracsoft against the MBS after-hours window. Flags claims where the appointment record places the consultation inside ordinary hours but Item 44 was billed. Also flags providers with Item 44 usage significantly above peer cohort. Timestamp discrepancies are the most directly provable finding in a Provider Review.

RULE · MBS-CO-CLAIM-721No Active Flags
CDM Item 721 Co-Claim Eligibility
GP Management Plan (Item 721) requires the patient to have a chronic condition expected to last ≥6 months. Validates frequency rules (max once per 12 months unless exceptional circumstance) and prevents 721/723 co-claim within minimum interval. Also checks that a valid Item 723 TCA is in Pracsoft before any allied health MBS billing occurs under that plan.
Source: MBS Online · CDM items 721–732Severity: MediumAuto-remediate: Yes — overdue review alert

A current Item 721 and a valid Item 723 referral must both be present and in-date. This rule catches the most common allied health billing error in multi-practitioner primary care settings.

Privacy Act · Data Architecture
MedicalGuard processes billing data from Pracsoft under a Data Processing Agreement. Patient identifiers are tokenised at the ingest boundary — no full name, DOB, or Medicare number is stored in the compliance layer. The token map is held in a separate, audit-logged keystore. All data remains in Australia (AWS ap-southeast-2 · Sydney). The June 2025 statutory tort reform means patients have direct legal action for serious privacy invasion. Health data breaches are the highest-risk category. NDB response runbook is current. 30-day OAIC countdown clock built in if a breach occurs.

Support · Knowledge Base
Help & FAQ
Answers to the most common questions about MedicalGuard — click any question to expand.
Getting Started
What does MedicalGuard connect to?▼
MedicalGuard connects read-only to your Pracsoft SQL database. It reads claim records, appointment metadata, provider details, and billing classifications every 15 minutes. It never writes back to Pracsoft.
Is patient data safe?▼
Patient identifiers (name, DOB, Medicare number) are replaced with opaque tokens at ingest. All data stored in Australia (AWS Sydney). Connection encrypted (TLS 1.3), stored data AES-256 encrypted. Full Data Processing Agreement provided before setup.
How long does setup take?▼
Pracsoft read-only connection: 2–4 hours. No software on clinical workstations. Practice manager training: 45-minute session. Most practices live within one week of signing.
How often is data updated?▼
Billing feed: every 15 minutes. Provider register and AHPRA registration: daily. MBS rule library: within 14 days of any MBS schedule change.
Compliance & Alerts
Why is a provider flagged when their billing seems correct?▼
A flag means the billing pattern sits outside the national peer benchmark — not that billing is wrong. Services Australia uses the same peer comparison. A flag is an early warning to review, not an accusation.
What is the peer benchmark?▼
Derived from Medicare published data on billing patterns for providers in the same specialty, location type, and patient demographic cohort. MedicalGuard flags providers in the top 10% of their peer group for any item.
What do I do when I receive an alert?▼
1. Click the alert to view full context. 2. Determine if there is a legitimate clinical explanation. 3. Document it via the Internal Audit workflow. 4. If a billing error is found, arrange correction through Pracsoft.
What is the 80/20 rule?▼
Under the Health Insurance Act 1973, a practitioner cannot provide more than 80% of services to patients of a single other practitioner in 12 months. MedicalGuard monitors this and flags it 90 days before the threshold is reached.
Technical
Can MedicalGuard change anything in Pracsoft?▼
No. The connection is strictly read-only. MedicalGuard cannot create, modify, or delete any record in Pracsoft.
What if the Pracsoft feed goes offline?▼
MedicalGuard displays the most recent data from the last successful sync and flags delayed feeds in amber on the dashboard. Alerts are not generated from stale data. Data is back-filled automatically on reconnection.
Revenue & Billing Optimiser
Is the revenue identified by IntelliSense legitimate?▼
Yes — every opportunity is a legitimate Medicare-funded entitlement the practice is not currently claiming. Not upcoding. IntelliSense will never recommend billing that carries Provider Review risk.
What data does IntelliSense use?▼
The practice's own Pracsoft data — patient demographics, chronic condition flags, care plan records, billing patterns, provider mix — combined with the current MBS schedule. All recommendations specific to this practice.
Allied Health
How does AHPRA monitoring work?▼
MedicalGuard checks each allied health practitioner's AHPRA registration against the public register daily. If a registration lapses or has conditions placed on it, an alert is generated immediately.
What is a TCA and why does it matter?▼
A Team Care Arrangement (TCA, Item 723) is required for patients to access Medicare rebates for allied health services. MedicalGuard cross-references every allied health billing event against the referring GP's Pracsoft record to confirm a valid TCA exists.

Reference · MBS Item Guide
MBS Glossary
Plain-English explanations of every MBS item and compliance term used throughout MedicalGuard.
GP Consultation Items
Item 3
Level A (brief) — attendance of brief nature. Peer benchmark: ~5% of consults.
Item 23
Level B (standard, <20 min) — the most common GP item. Peer benchmark: ~50–60% of consults.
Item 36
Level C (long, ≥20 min) — requires documented duration in clinical records. Peer benchmark: ~20%. PSR threshold: top 10% of peers.
Item 44
After-hours consultation — outside ordinary hours. Timestamp must match Pracsoft records. Peer benchmark: ~8%.
Telehealth
Item 91890
Telehealth phone (≥20 min) — Level C equivalent via telephone. Existing clinical relationship required.
Chronic Disease Management
Item 721
GP Management Plan (GPMP) — for patients with a chronic condition. Once per 12-month period. Annual review via Item 725.
Item 723
Team Care Arrangement (TCA) — coordinates GP with 2+ other treating providers. Required for allied health Medicare rebates. Valid 12 months.
Item 725
GPMP review — annual review of an existing GPMP.
Health Assessments
Item 701
Short health assessment (<30 min) — once per calendar year per patient.
Item 715
Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander health assessment — annual health check for eligible patients.
Item 10004
75+ health assessment — once per 12 months.
Bulk Billing & Incentives
Item 10990
Triple bulk billing incentive — additional payment for bulk billing children under 16, concession card holders, and Commonwealth seniors. One of the most under-claimed items.
Item 75870
Bulk billing incentive — outer metropolitan — higher rate for eligible patients in ASGC-RA 2 areas.
Item 75871
Bulk billing incentive — regional/remote — highest rate for eligible patients in regional and remote areas.
Allied Health Items
Items 10953–10970
Allied health services under CDM — Medicare rebates for physio, dietitian, psychology, podiatry, OT, exercise physiology. Require current TCA (Item 723).
Item 10960
Physiotherapy — initial assessment under TCA — must have current Item 723 referral.
Compliance Terms
PSR
Professional Services Review — independent body investigating Medicare practitioners with potentially inappropriate billing.
Peer benchmark
Median billing pattern for practitioners in the same specialty and geographic cohort. MedicalGuard flags providers in the top 10% for any item.
Provider Review
Formal Services Australia investigation. Triggered by data-matching, complaint, or PSR referral. Documented internal audit evidence is the most effective response.
80/20 rule
Health Insurance Act: a practitioner cannot provide more than 80% of services to patients of a single other practitioner in 12 months.
AHPRA
Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency — national registration body. MedicalGuard checks allied health registrations daily.
MHTP
Mental Health Treatment Plan — created by GP for Medicare-rebated psychology sessions. Up to 10 sessions per calendar year.
CDM
Chronic Disease Management — Medicare framework covering Items 721, 723, 725 and allied health items.
